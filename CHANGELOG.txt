TEXTURE LAYER MANAGER - CHANGELOG
==================================

1.0.0 (June 2026) - first public release
- Six layer types: Paint, Fill, Procedural, Adjustment, Group,
  Reference.
- 24 procedural patterns, all parametric, generated in 3D space
  (no UV unwrap required) with optional UV / triplanar projection.
- Per-channel output routing: Base Color, Roughness, Metallic,
  Emission, Transmission, Alpha - plus secondary PBR channels
  (normal, bump) per layer.
- Per-channel blend modes (19 colour modes; 41 math operations on
  the Alpha channel).
- Mask pipeline: 11 sources (geometry, light, patterns), two-source
  combine, falloff refinement.
- Material extras: IOR, volume absorption / scatter, real geometric
  displacement, alpha pipeline, keyframable layer opacity.
- Live hot updates: sliders edit the node graph in place, no rebuild.
- 13 included preset materials (.tlm), loadable and fully editable.
- Bake / export of composited channels.
